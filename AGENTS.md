# Workspace conventions

- Keep shared assets, configuration, outputs and scripts at the workspace root. Put each scene implementation in `examples/<name>/`; reusable Python utilities belong in `src/mujoco_tools/`.
- Keep the generated canonical scene at `examples/<name>/scene.xml`. Keep new raw collection datasets in `outputs/datasets/<name>/run_<timestamp>/`, or the explicit `--output` directory. Each raw episode must have its own `episode_<seed>/` subdirectory containing same-stem HDF5, YAML, XML and a single head-camera MP4. The ten-episode fixed-position batch is `outputs/datasets/insert_plug/test/`. Store execution results and diagnostics in HDF5 metadata rather than separate JSON, PNG or plan files. Store `model_path` and `execution_result.model` as the XML filename relative to the episode directory so the batch can be moved together.
- The current task is `insert_plug`. Expert targets and synchronized RGB-D/state/action recording both default to 10 Hz, with each command held for 100 ms and physics at 1 kHz. No hidden higher-rate target interpolation is used. Distinguish this aligned timing from legacy 50 Hz control / 10 or 15 Hz recording. Fixed holder positions are the default; randomization must be requested.
- The current expert uses `continuous_hover=true`: source approach → descent and target transfer → alignment → shallow insertion share nonzero downward waypoint velocities (0.02 m/s at source, 0.008 m/s at target) through quintic Hermite position segments. Do not reintroduce hover tracking holds or stopped alignment correction loops in this mode. Target continuation measures the actual held transform but anchors commands to the previous target/FK and incoming velocity, not the servo-lagged actual pose. The immediate entry-fit check remains; an invalid fit reports failure. `continuous_hover=false` retains the legacy stopped-correction path.
- Current heights are `approach_height=0.04` relative to the grasp point, `transport_height=0.10` as absolute TCP world height, and `planning.retract_height=0.05` relative to the shallow-insertion TCP. There is no hidden default 0.07 m extraction-rise floor. Shorter extraction uses `cosine_velocity`, `extraction_duration=4.0`, `extraction_ramp_fraction=0.3`. Preserve 200 ms stable opposed grasp with original drift limit and at most 0.8 s extra waiting, actual grasp/shallow-insertion endpoint tracking, extraction clearance, 12 mm insertion depth, 1 s release, and final 250 ms stable-success checks. `continuous_free_space` still skips extra extraction/retraction settling. To meet the user's under-20-second target on the verified layouts, current stage minima are descent 1.0 s, transfer 1.5 s, alignment 1.2 s, insertion 2.5 s; retiming may extend them. Joint speed/acceleration bounds are 1.4 rad/s and 4 rad/s², Cartesian bounds 0.18 m/s and 0.8 m/s². Only empty-hand approach has a 0.24 m/s speed bound. Do not meet the duration target by deleting physical grasp/fit/release/final-stability requirements.

The two holders are already fixed world bodies with no joints; do not add artificial mass, welds or per-frame resets. - Keep other explicitly requested images, dual-view videos and plan exports under `outputs/<name>/`, separate from dataset directories. Preserve recordings, user STL files and reusable assets during routine cleanup; remove obsolete generated previews and diagnostics when authorized.

- Keep environment and dependency configuration at the root; use the shared Conda environment `mujoco`.
- LeRobot conversion uses the isolated `lerobot` Conda environment with its own root-level environment/lock files. Do not change simulator dependencies to satisfy LeRobot's Python or PyTorch requirements.
- Trained-policy deployment uses the isolated `mujoco_policy` Conda environment and the local `third_party/lerobot` fork. The scene-independent ACT adapter is `src/mujoco_tools/lerobot_policy.py`; task execution is `examples/insert_plug/rollout.py`, configured by `config/insert_plug_policy.yaml`. Preserve checkpoint files and their saved normalization statistics. Evaluation outputs belong under `outputs/insert_plug/rollouts/`, never in training datasets. Deployment now defaults to `stop_on_contact=false` and `contact_diagnostics=false`: no `ReplayContactMonitor` or contact-based interruption. Success means actual insertion/release/withdrawal and 250 ms stability; a diagnostic contact count alone must not reject success. Explicit `--contact-diagnostics` records only; `--stop-on-contact` enables legacy strict stopping. Disabled diagnostics yield `contact_clean=null` and zero contact checks, while `physics_checks` counts actual task-success sampling. Retain invalid-action/numerical checks, 10 Hz held actions and 1 kHz physics; do not silently clip model actions or substitute expert/planner corrections.
